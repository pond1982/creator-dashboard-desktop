Interactive demo

Explore the dashboard.

Choose a sample channel, inspect its daily views, or switch to the App Store to compare sales.

Your channels, up close

See subscribers and lifetime views for each channel. Sort up to 25 recent uploads by views, likes or comments. Connect private Studio analytics for watch time, subscriber changes, traffic sources and viewer countries.

Know which apps are selling

Rank paid downloads, free downloads, in-app purchases or estimated proceeds. Compare 1, 7, 30 or 90 reporting days with the previous period, and choose the currency you want to see.

Native Mac widgets

Keep your numbers in view.

Add a widget for the number you check most. Pin several for different channels, or click one to open its place in the app.

Small, medium or large. Add them in Notification Center on macOS 13, or on your desktop with per-widget channel selection on macOS 14 and later.

On your computer

Built for your daily check-in.

↻

Updates in the background

Keep the app in the Mac menu bar or Windows tray. While online, it checks public YouTube data every 30 minutes and private Studio reports every six hours.

PDF

Save a report to share

Export a PDF for one channel or your selected sales period and currency. Keep a copy of this month’s numbers or send a snapshot to a collaborator.

Keep your data local

Your computer fetches reports directly from Google and Apple. Your operating system protects saved credentials, and reports stay in your user profile.

Mac & Windows

Set up your own dashboard.

Choose YouTube, App Store Connect, or both. Add the channels you follow and choose Midnight, Aurora or Daylight for your desktop.

Choose your installer

macOS 13 Ventura or later

In the release assets, choose the mac-arm64.dmg for an Apple M-series chip or mac-x64.dmg for an Intel Mac. Open it, drag Argus Pip to Applications, and launch it there.

Windows 10 or 11 · x64

Choose the win-x64.exe release asset for a 64-bit Intel or AMD computer. Run the installer, then open Argus Pip from the Start menu.

Connect your accounts

Add a Google API key for public channel stats. Authorize each channel in your browser for private Studio reports. Apple sales use your team’s reporting key and vendor number.
